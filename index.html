      GAIA — a living Earth

      You are looking at a miniature world. Thousands of simulated lives rise, work, love, sleep and dream across continents. Each region follows its own culture, rhythm, and time of day.

      Type anything into the prompt at the bottom to shape the world. The simulation understands natural language across many themes:

      • Disasters: earthquake, tsunami, volcano, flood, drought, hurricane, wildfire, blizzard, storm
      • Blessings: bless, prosper, peace, renaissance, golden age, miracle, healing
      • Society: festival, protest, war, revolution, migration, baby boom, exodus
      • Health: plague, cure, pandemic, vaccine
      • Wonder: aurora, eclipse, UFO, alien contact, nightfall, sunrise, snow, rainbow

      Specify a place with prepositions — in Japan, over Europe, across Africa, worldwide — or let Gaia choose.

      Click any country for details. Use the time controls to speed up life itself.

      No servers. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
