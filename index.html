          GAIA — a living Earth

          You are looking at a miniature Earth. Thousands of simulated lives rise, work, love, sleep and dream across continents. Each region follows its own culture, rhythm, and time of day.

          Type anything into the prompt at the bottom to shape the world. Gaia understands natural language across many themes:

          • Disasters: earthquake, tsunami, volcano, flood, drought, hurricane, wildfire, blizzard, meteor, nuclear
          • Blessings: bless, prosper, peace, renaissance, golden age, miracle, healing, silk road
          • Society: festival, protest, war, revolution, migration, baby boom, exodus, election, olympics
          • Health: plague, cure, pandemic, vaccine
          • Wonder: aurora, eclipse, UFO, alien contact, nightfall, sunrise, snow, rainbow, dragons, zombies

          Specify a place — in Japan, over Europe, across Africa, worldwide, in the middle east — or let Gaia choose for you.

          Time travel. Drag the timeline at the bottom-left, click a gold mark to jump to a famous moment, or type:

          • travel to 1969 · go to year 1492 · take me to the renaissance
          • travel to the black death · go to 500 bc · travel to 2150

          Ask Gaia. Try status, how is the world, or just hello gaia.

          Buttons. 🌐 toggles between a flat map and a rotating globe. ◎ cycles the data layer (mood, happiness, peace, prosperity, health, climate, population). 🏆 shows achievements.

          Shortcuts. / focus the prompt · ↑/↓ browse prompt history · space pause/play · h this help · r reset view · esc close panels · click clock copy share link.

          No servers. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser. Built with D3 + TopoJSON and an unreasonable amount of care.
